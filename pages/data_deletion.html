Runaway: Sky Towers – Account and Data Deletion Request

We understand you wish to permanently delete your personal data associated with your **Runaway: Sky Towers** game account. To comply with data privacy policies and ensure all your information is removed, please follow the steps below.

1. The Core Deletion Process (In-Game)

The fastest and most complete way to delete the data stored on our servers is through the game itself:

  1. Launch Runaway: Sky Towers: Open the game application on your device.
  2. Go to Settings: Navigate to the **Settings** menu (top-left corner of main screen), then visit the **Privacy** tab.
  3. Find the Deletion Button: Locate and tap the **Delete User Data** button (bottom of the screen).
  4. Confirm: Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm the permanent deletion.

This action will **immediately and permanently remove** the following data that we control:

  • Your Game Username (used for saved games)
  • Your Saved Game Data (including any cloud saves tied to your profile)
  • Your Unique Game Profile ID

2. Important Note on Multiple Installations

ACTION REQUIRED: If you have installed **Runaway: Sky Towers** on multiple devices, please follow these steps before requesting deletion:
  • Uninstall the Game from all devices except the one you are currently using to perform the deletion.
This ensures that no older data or temporary credentials are left active, guaranteeing a clean and complete removal of your profile.

3. Data from External Services (Google Play)

For data collected by and stored on external platforms (Google Play Services), the deletion process is managed by those services. We cannot remove this data on your behalf.

Data Type Who Manages the Data? How to Delete It
In-App Purchase History Google Play To delete your purchase history, you must delete your entire Google Play Account. We cannot delete this financial data, as it is retained by Google for legal and tax compliance.
Public Leaderboard Scores Google Play Games Services (GPGS) To delete your scores from the public leaderboards, you must follow Google's steps to delete data for *Runaway: Sky Towers* within your Google Play Games settings. We cannot delete this data directly.
Anonymous Analytics Google Analytics Runaway: Sky Towers collects anonymous event data for general performance tracking. This data is not linked to any personal identifier, making it impossible to single out and delete on an individual basis.
Game Achievements/Metadata Google Play Games Services (GPGS) You can manage or delete your GPGS profile and associated achievements through your Google Play settings.

4. Need Help?

If you encounter any issues accessing the in-game deletion feature, or if you have any questions about this process, please contact our support team at runawayskytowers@gmail.com.
