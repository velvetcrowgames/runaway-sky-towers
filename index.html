Runaway: Sky Towers

A fast-paced endless runner where only the sharpest reflexes survive!

Game Description

Runaway: Sky Towers is a thrilling, fast-paced endless runner where you guide a mechanical ball across a floating 3D platform. With simple, intuitive controls—just tap left or right—players must dodge obstacles, enemies, and collect coins and power-ups as the speed gradually increases. The challenge never ends, pushing players to their limits for pure, reflex-based fun.

Key Features

  • Procedurally generated endless levels – every run is unique, offering limitless replayability.
  • Simple controls – tap left or right for instant, accessible gameplay.
  • Gradual speed ramp – start easy and build up to intense, high-speed action.
  • Collect coins and powerful power-ups like wings, rockets, magnets, and coin doublers.
  • Global leaderboards – compete with friends and players worldwide.
  • Cloud saves – keep your progress safe across devices.
  • Offline mode – play anywhere, anytime, and sync your progress later.
  • Unique stylized graphics – a vibrant comic look with bold outlines and a striking red/green color contrast.
